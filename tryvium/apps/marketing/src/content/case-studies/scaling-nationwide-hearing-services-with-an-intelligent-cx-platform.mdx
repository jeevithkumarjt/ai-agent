## About the National Hearing Test

The National Hearing Test (NHT) is a nationally recognized public health initiative focused on providing accessible hearing screening services.

It is not a typical screening. It uses scientifically validated speech-in-noise testing to help individuals assess whether they may benefit from a comprehensive clinical hearing evaluation.

Designed for broad accessibility, the National Hearing Test is available nationwide through both landline and mobile phones, making hearing screening convenient and widely accessible.

As participation grew, the organization required a more scalable and reliable engagement platform capable of supporting nationwide demand while maintaining a consistent user experience.

## What prevented the National Hearing Test from scaling further?

The existing technology platform faced limitations that impacted scalability and service delivery.

### Key challenges included:

- Limited capacity to handle large volumes of concurrent callers
- Risk of dropped calls once capacity limits were reached
- Restricted operational flexibility and control
- Inconsistent call quality affecting user experience
- Limited visibility into interaction data and program performance

To continue expanding its reach and improve service reliability, the organization needed a modern customer engagement platform that could scale seamlessly while delivering a high-quality experience for every participant.

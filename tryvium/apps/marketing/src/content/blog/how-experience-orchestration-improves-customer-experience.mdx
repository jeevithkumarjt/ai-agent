## What Is Experience Orchestration?

Experience Orchestration is orchestrating an experience across all touchpoints — bringing together the best of customer interactions, AI agents, human agents, enterprise applications and business workflows to deliver a seamless experience.

An Experience Orchestration Platform is different from other conventional customer service platforms that generally send a conversation to the appropriate conversational agent. It keeps context across every interaction, automates the workflow, prescribes next best action and involves human experts in the decision only when required.

Where traditional service platforms are built to route conversations, an Experience Orchestration Platform is built to manage the entire journey. It carries context across every interaction, automates the workflows behind the scenes, recommends the next best action, and brings in human expertise only when a decision genuinely needs it.

AI-led Experience Orchestration combines autonomous AI with human expertise to improve service quality while ensuring governance and oversight. AI agents handle routine tasks and execute approved actions, while human experts focus on complex decisions, exceptions, and validation. The result is a connected, personalized customer experience across every channel.

## Why Traditional Customer Experience Strategies Are No Longer Enough

Traditional customer experience strategies have focused on expanding communication channels and introducing automation. However, many organizations still rely on disconnected systems where IVRs, chatbots, ticketing platforms, and contact centers operate independently.

Most traditional CX strategies have grown by addition — one more channel, one more automation layer — without solving the underlying problem: IVRs, chatbots, ticketing platforms, and contact centers still largely operate as separate systems.

As a result, customers often repeat information, wait through multiple transfers, and lose conversation history when switching channels. Instead of resolving issues, automation frequently serves as a gateway before escalating requests to a human agent.

This disconnected approach increases customer effort, slows resolution times, and makes it difficult to deliver consistent omnichannel customer experiences. Modern organizations need a service model that connects every interaction, preserves context, and enables AI and human teams to work together throughout the customer journey.

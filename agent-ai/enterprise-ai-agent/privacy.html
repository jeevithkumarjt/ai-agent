9. Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

This DPA applies when Tryvium processes personal data on behalf of a customer (the “Controller”), including chat conversations, knowledge base content, and analytics data within the Services.

  • Role. The customer is the Controller; Tryvium is the Processor, and Tryvium’s subprocessors are Sub-Processors.
  • Instructions. We process data only on documented instructions from the Controller, unless required by law (in which case we inform the Controller first, where permitted).
  • Confidentiality. Personnel authorized to process data are subject to confidentiality and data protection obligations.
  • Security. We implement the technical and organizational measures described in Section 8, including encryption of data in transit and at rest.
  • Sub-processors. A list of sub-processors (hosting, LLM/embedding providers, observability) is available on request; the Controller is notified of changes and may object in writing.
  • Data Subject Rights. We assist the Controller in responding to data subject requests under GDPR Articles 15–22, to the extent technically possible.
  • International Transfers. Transfers outside the EEA/UK are covered by SCCs or other appropriate safeguards per Section 7.
  • Deletion. Upon termination, we delete or return personal data within the agreed retention period unless law requires retention.
  • Audit. Upon written request and reasonable notice, we make available information demonstrating compliance with this DPA, subject to confidentiality.
  • Liability. Each party’s liability under this DPA is subject to the liability provisions of the underlying customer contract.

To request a countersigned DPA or the current sub-processor list, contact legal@tryvium.ai.

## Customer Overview

A global consumer goods company with over 30,000 employees needed to streamline their annual benefits enrollment and employee communication process. The annual enrollment period created massive spikes in HR inquiries, overwhelming the support team.

## What challenges were impacting service operations?

The annual benefits enrollment period generated tens of thousands of employee inquiries, overwhelming the HR support team and leading to long wait times. Employees were frustrated by the inability to get quick answers about their benefits options. The HR team was spending months each year on repetitive inquiries instead of strategic initiatives.

## How did Tryvium simplify CX?

Tryvium deployed AI agents to handle benefits-related inquiries, guide employees through enrollment, and escalate complex cases to HR specialists with full context. The platform integrated with existing HR systems and Microsoft Teams to provide seamless employee experiences.

## What changed after implementing Tryvium?

Employee satisfaction with the enrollment process increased significantly. Average enrollment time decreased, and the HR team was able to focus on strategic initiatives rather than repetitive inquiries. The platform now handles over 150,000 employee interactions annually with high satisfaction rates.

"Tryvium has transformed our employee support operations. Our HR team can now focus on strategic initiatives while AI agents handle the majority of routine inquiries efficiently."

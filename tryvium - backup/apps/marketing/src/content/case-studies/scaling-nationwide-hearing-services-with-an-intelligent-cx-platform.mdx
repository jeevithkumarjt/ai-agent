## About the National Hearing Test

A nationwide hearing services provider needed to scale their customer engagement platform to support rapid geographic expansion. The organization provides hearing assessment services across hundreds of locations nationwide.

## What prevented the National Hearing Test from scaling further?

As the company expanded to new locations, their legacy contact center could not keep up with growing inquiry volume. Long wait times, missed appointments, and inconsistent service quality were impacting patient satisfaction and business growth.

## How did Tryvium help streamline the hearing assessment experience?

Tryvium deployed AI-powered patient engagement across all locations. AI agents handle appointment scheduling, insurance verification, follow-up communications, and frequently asked questions. The platform integrates with the provider's scheduling and CRM systems.

## What changed after implementing Tryvium?

Patient engagement grew significantly. Appointment no-show rates dropped substantially. The provider was able to scale to over 200 locations without proportional increases in support staff. AI agents now handle the majority of all patient inquiries end-to-end.

## To conclude

The National Hearing Test transformed their patient engagement model with Tryvium's AI-orchestrated platform, enabling nationwide scaling without compromising service quality.

## Customer Overview

A leading global pharmaceutical company needed to modernize their employee support operations. With a distributed workforce and complex IT and HR service needs, they required a solution that could deliver personalized, efficient support at scale across multiple regions.

## What challenges were impacting remote support operations?

The company's existing service desk was struggling with low chat adoption rates, long resolution times, and inconsistent service quality. Employees frequently had to repeat information when transitioning between self-service and agent-assisted support. The IT and HR teams were overwhelmed with repetitive inquiries, limiting their capacity for strategic work.

## How did Tryvium enable global employee support at scale?

Tryvium deployed its Experience Orchestration Platform integrated with Microsoft Teams and ServiceNow. AI agents began handling IT and HR inquiries directly within Teams, providing instant resolutions for common requests. For complex issues, AI agents seamlessly escalated to human experts with full context preserved.

## To conclude

The results were transformative. Chat adoption increased by 90% within three months. Resolution times improved by 40%. Employee satisfaction scores reached record highs. AI agents now handle the majority of all IT and HR interactions end-to-end, freeing the service desk team to focus on strategic initiatives.

## Introduction

Experience orchestration is transforming how enterprises deliver service across customer and employee interactions. Here are ten powerful use cases and the business benefits they deliver.

## What Is Experience Orchestration?

Experience orchestration is an AI-led approach to managing customer and employee interactions. Unlike traditional automation that follows linear paths, orchestration creates a unified layer that coordinates AI agents, human experts, and enterprise systems in real time.

## Why Enterprises Invest in Experience Orchestration

Enterprises invest in experience orchestration to reduce operational costs, improve customer satisfaction, increase employee productivity, and gain a competitive advantage through superior service experiences.

## 10 Experience Orchestration Use Cases & Their Benefits

### 1. Customer Service Resolution

AI agents handle end-to-end customer inquiries, reducing handle times by up to 60% and improving CSAT scores through consistent, personalized interactions.

### 2. IT Service Desk Automation

Automate password resets, software access requests, and common troubleshooting — freeing IT teams for higher-value work.

### 3. HR Service Delivery

Employee inquiries about benefits, payroll, and policies are handled instantly by AI agents with seamless escalation to HR specialists.

### 4. Order Management

Customers can check order status, modify orders, and initiate returns through conversational AI with real-time system access.

### 5. Knowledge Management

AI agents provide instant access to enterprise knowledge bases, delivering accurate answers from across the organization.

### 6. Employee Onboarding

New employees receive guided onboarding experiences with AI agents handling FAQs, paperwork, and training schedules.

### 7. Field Service Support

Technicians access real-time information, schematics, and expert guidance through AI-powered conversational interfaces.

### 8. Compliance and Audit Support

AI agents guide employees through compliance requirements and maintain audit trails for regulatory reporting.

### 9. Partner and Channel Support

Extend AI-orchestrated support to partners and channel organizations for consistent brand experiences.

### 10. Customer Feedback and Insights

AI agents proactively gather customer feedback and surface actionable insights for continuous improvement.

## Conclusion

The common thread across all use cases: AI agents orchestrate the full resolution lifecycle, with humans participating only when their unique judgment is needed.

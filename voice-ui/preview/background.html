Background System

Module 2 · aurora mesh · noise · cursor light · particles — move your mouse, scroll, toggle the theme

An ambient stage for the Tryvium voice experience.

The aurora drifts on incommensurate sine cycles so no two moments look the same. Grain sits on top, unanimated. A soft accent light follows the pointer and fades when you stop.

This page is the preview harness only — the background lives behind the hero (M3) and the whole widget in production.

Glass panel over aurora Content sits at z-index 1, above the fixed ambient layer. Scroll to see the layer stay put while content moves.

Performance notes for review: 4 blurred layers animate via transform only (no per-frame paint), particles share one pre-rendered soft-dot sprite on a single canvas, noise is a static SVG data-URI, and the whole module pauses when out of view or the tab is hidden. Reduced-motion renders one static frame.

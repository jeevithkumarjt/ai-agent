FAQ

Common questions.

How fast can we go live?

Most teams route their first call within 48 hours. We import your workflows, train the voice on your docs, and hand you a test line before you switch over.

How does the human handoff work?

When a caller asks for a person (or intent confidence drops), the session escalates with a full transcript, detected intent and any captured data — so the agent never asks the caller to repeat anything.

Is our data used to train shared models?

No. Your calls stay in your tenant. Transcripts and model context are yours; nothing is used to train models for other customers.

Does it work with our existing phone system?

Yes. We connect over SIP, PSTN or your existing telephony provider, and route to your CRM or helpdesk in real time.

# Why Northwind

Northwind is a fictional experience-orchestration company used as sample content for
this repository. It exists so the knowledge base can be ingested, retrieved and cited
without depending on anybody else's material.

## What the company does

Northwind builds software that lets support and sales teams run customer conversations
from one place. The product line covers an employee help desk, a customer service desk
and workflow automation for routing requests.

## Mission

Make every customer conversation traceable. Every answer should be able to point back
to the document it came from, and every unanswered question should be reported as
unanswered rather than guessed.

## Company facts

- Founded as an internal tool for a support team, later split out as a product.
- Headquarters is not publicly relevant for this demo.
- The company operates in the customer support and service-management software market.

## Product areas

- **Employee help desk** — internal ticketing for staff-facing requests.
- **Customer service desk** — outbound support queues with SLA tracking.
- **Automation** — rules that route, escalate and summarise conversations.
- **Analytics** — volume, response time and resolution reporting.

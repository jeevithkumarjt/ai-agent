# Northwind Pricing

Sample pricing content for the demo knowledge base. None of this is a real offer.

## Plans

- **Standard** — per-seat monthly billing. Help desk, service desk and standard
  reporting. Email support during business hours.
- **Enterprise** — annual contract. Adds SSO, audit logs, a dedicated success contact
  and 24/7 support.
- **Custom** — for teams above 500 seats or with data-residency requirements. Priced
  per contract.

## Billing notes

- Seats are counted per active user per month. Inactive seats are not billed.
- Enterprise and Custom plans are invoiced annually.
- Onboarding and migration assistance are quoted separately.

## Trial

A 14-day trial is available on the Standard plan. It does not include SSO or
audit logs.
